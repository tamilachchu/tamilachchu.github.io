# Tamil Achchu — Early Tamil Print Database

This repository hosts the Early Tamil Print Database, published at [tamilachchu.github.io](https://tamilachchu.github.io). The database is a bibliographical record of Tamil books printed between 1832 and 1878, most of them at Cen̲n̲apaṭṭaṇam (Madras) and many associated with the scholar-editor Caravaṇapperumāḷaiyar and the Kalvi Viḷakkam press. Each title is linked to the persons who wrote, commented on or edited it and to the firm that printed it, so the collection can be searched and browsed by title, person or press. Names and titles are given in romanised form as recorded in the source catalogues, and the search ignores diacritics and common spelling differences. The project is managed by Shibi Laxman Kumaraperumal, a doctoral candidate in the University of Toronto's Department of History, with collaborative affiliations in South Asian Studies and Book History and Print Culture.
